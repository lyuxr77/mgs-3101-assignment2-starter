II. Descriptive Patterns Found
1. Symmetrical Distribution of Order Values and Costs
	The overall average order value is €113,361.74, with a median of €105,419.43. The mean of cost is €94,369.31, with a median of €87,094.76. Because the mean and median values are relatively close, the financial data exhibits a balance distribution without extreme skewness. 
2. Different category has different performance
	Different category reveals distinct business contribution. For example, Clothing is the volume leader, which accounts for the highest transaction count with 155 orders, and its average order values €113,946.34. While Accessories is the value leader, which achieves the highest average order value at €134398.02 across 35 orders. 
3. Major Orders Concentrated on PC Devices
	Looking at the highest order values and large enterprise orders are mainly processed on PC devices. And the single largest order in the dataset: €383,996.76, with a cost of €304,701.43 made in the UK was also using a PC.

III. Threshold Evaluation & Justification
	I chose €100,000 as the threshold because I thought keeping the average order value above €100,000 helps cover operational expenses, sales commissions, and shipping costs for commercial retail and business sales. And the result shows a PASS of this database, which means the market runs well.
